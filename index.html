About

Oversees creative production, PR, and sport presentation for the Yomiuri Giants' home games — spanning large-scale ceremonies to year-round in-stadium fan content and events. Her work extends internationally, including brand and media relations for the 2026 World Baseball Classic™ Tokyo Pool, and planning and operations for the MLB Tokyo Series opening game.
